# Plus Flat BTO Timeline: From Application to Key Collection

## Introduction

A Plus flat is a decade-long commitment, and the calendar you plan around is longer than the wait for keys. What helps most is knowing the order HDB moves you through, what you pay at each step, and what you can't do once you move in.

The Plus flat BTO timeline runs from application to queue position, booking, lease signing within nine months, then key collection once the flat is ready. Plus flats carry a 10-year MOP, against five years for Standard flats, with no whole-flat rental during it.

Below, we map the milestones and payments, and explain when the Plus MOP clock starts (two HDB pages word it differently). This guide assumes you already hold a valid HFE letter. If you haven't cleared that step, start with the [HDB flat eligibility requirements](https://hometheory.sg/blog/hdb-flat-eligibility-hfe-letter-application/).

## 1. The Plus flat journey: application to key collection

Start with the label. HDB sorts new flats into Standard, Plus and Prime. Plus flats sit in choicer locations, come with more subsidies and carry tighter sale conditions. The framework applies to flats launched from the October 2024 sales exercise onward, and HDB announces each project's classification at launch. So check the label before you apply. [MyNiceHome's flat types guide](https://www.mynicehome.gov.sg/get-started/hdb-flat-types-classification-guide/) lays out the framework, and we cover the Standard vs Plus vs Prime BTO differences that matter for timing below.

Now the sequence. It runs in five steps:

1. You apply online through the HDB Flat Portal. A valid HFE letter comes first.
2. HDB tells you your queue position.
3. You book a flat when your turn comes.
4. You sign the Agreement for Lease.
5. You collect your keys once the booked flat is ready.

The February 2026 exercise shows how one launch played out. It is a worked example, not a rule for every exercise. Applications ran from 4 to 11 February 2026. HDB informed applicants of queue positions in March 2026, and flat booking was tentatively set to start from July 2026. HDB also shortlists applicants beyond 100% and potentially up to 200% of the flat supply, so a queue position doesn't guarantee a flat.

You'll notice we haven't given a total wait in years. That's deliberate. HDB's published material gives no duration from booking or lease signing to key collection. It says only that you're invited to collect keys when the flat is ready, and you can check building progress on the MyHDB Page. So be careful with any single number quoted elsewhere.

Ballot odds, second-timer reapplication and the resale levy sit in our guide on [reapplying for a BTO as a second-timer](https://hometheory.sg/blog/mop-fulfilled-reapply-bto-second-timer/), so we won't repeat them here.

## 2. Payment milestones mapped to the HDB process
<!-- info-gain anchor: angle 1 -->

So why does the sequence matter for your wallet? Because every milestone has a payment attached, and they don't arrive evenly. Here is how the cash moves, based on HDB's administrative details for the February 2026 exercise and its Key Collection page.

| Milestone | What you pay |
|---|---|
| Flat booking | Option fee: $500 (2-room Flexi), $1,000 (3-room) or $2,000 (4-room, 5-room, 3Gen, Executive). It forms part of your downpayment. |
| Agreement for Lease | Downpayment of 10% (HDB loan) or 20% (financial institution loan), paid by CPF and/or cash. |
| Key collection | Balance purchase price in full, plus survey fee. Registration fees and stamp duty on the Deed of Assignment may apply. |

HDB invites you to sign the Agreement for Lease within nine months after you book. 

Young couples who qualify for deferred income assessment can pay less at signing. HDB's figure is 2.5% (from the June 2024 exercise), with the balance payable when the flat is ready for key collection. That relief only shifts the timing. The full balance still lands before you get your keys, so plan for it early.

The last payment step is key collection. Per [HDB's Key Collection page](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/process-for-buying-a-new-flat/key-collection), the balance purchase price must be paid in full before you collect. The survey fee is payable at collection. If a resale levy applies to you, it is due before key collection too (our second-timer guide covers how that works).



## 3. When does the Plus 10-year MOP clock start?
<!-- info-gain anchor: angle 2 -->

The MOP is the Minimum Occupation Period, the stretch when you must live in the flat and can't sell it. For new and resale Plus and Prime flats, HDB sets it at 10 years. Standard and unclassified flats have five years, and the Fresh Start Housing Scheme has 20. The exact length for your flat depends on your purchase mode, flat type and date of application. So the HDB Plus flat MOP 10 years rule is the headline, not the whole story.

Here is the part most guides skip: when does that clock start? Two HDB-owned pages give different answers.

- MyNiceHome says the MOP "is calculated from the date you collect the keys to your flat."
- HDB's [Conditions After Buying a New Flat](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/conditions-after-buying-a-new-flat) page says the MOP "starts from the legal completion date of your flat purchase."

We can't tell you which one governs your flat, and we won't guess. What both pages agree on is what the count leaves out. It excludes any period when you don't physically live in the flat, such as when the whole flat is rented out (HDB's page adds "with HDB's prior approval") or when the flat lease has been infringed.

So what should you do? Don't count from a calendar guess. MyNiceHome points to HDB's Check Flat Details e-Service for checking whether you have met the MOP on your own flat. If your dates matter for a decision, such as when you could sell or apply again, confirm them with HDB directly.

## 4. Life during the MOP and subsidy recovery on resale

During the MOP, you cannot:

- sell the flat
- rent out the whole flat
- buy a private residential property
- apply for another BTO flat

You can still rent out spare bedrooms, subject to eligibility.

Core family members must stay on the application and physically live in the flat. Their names cannot be removed. Core members also can't apply or be listed in an HFE letter during the MOP. If you don't occupy the flat, HDB can impose a financial penalty, and the most serious cases can end in compulsory acquisition.

There is also a cost when you eventually sell. On selling a Plus flat bought from HDB, you return to HDB a percentage of the resale price or valuation, whichever is higher. HDB's page states the rule without giving the percentage, so check the current figure with HDB before you build it into a plan. Plus recovery is less than Prime, per MyNiceHome.

If you already own a flat, you must dispose of it within six months of key collection.

## 5. Alternative options if you'd rather not wait

Sale of Balance Flats is a different route, and the timeline changes with it. If you book a flat that is already completed, you must sign the Agreement for Lease and collect your keys within nine months of booking.

Compare that with BTO, where keys are collected only when the booked flat is ready. For eligibility and how the exercise works, read our guide to [Sale of Balance Flats eligibility](https://hometheory.sg/blog/sale-of-balance-flats-sbf-eligibility/).

## 6. FAQ

### What is the average waiting time for BTO flats in 2026?

We don't quote a single figure, because HDB's process pages don't publish one. HDB states that keys are collected when the booked flat is ready. We also found no Plus-specific waiting time in HDB's process pages. Check the launch details for your project instead.

### What is the typical downpayment timeline for a BTO flat?

You pay in stages. The option fee ($500 to $2,000, depending on flat type) is due at booking. The 10% or 20% downpayment is due when you sign the Agreement for Lease, which HDB invites within nine months after booking. The balance follows before key collection.

### How long does the Plus flat BTO timeline take?

HDB doesn't give a duration from booking to key collection, so the honest answer is that it depends on when your flat is ready. What you can plan around is the sequence: application, queue position, booking, lease signing, key collection. Then the 10-year MOP applies, and its exact start date (key collection or legal completion, depending on which HDB page you read) is worth confirming with HDB.

### What is the typical timeline for BTO key collection?

HDB invites you to collect keys when they're ready, and you can follow building progress on the MyHDB Page. You pay the balance purchase price in full before you collect, and the survey fee when you collect. Registration fees and stamp duty on the Deed of Assignment may also apply.

## Conclusion

Treat the Plus timeline as a sequence of milestones, each with a payment or a rule attached. Check the project's classification before you apply, budget for the downpayment and key collection costs early, and confirm your MOP start date with HDB instead of assuming it.
